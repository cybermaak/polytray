Built for big, messy model libraries

⚡ Fast scanning & search

Point Polytray at your folders; it indexes everything into a local database and keeps it fresh with file watching. Search and filter thousands of models instantly.

🖼 Automatic thumbnails

Background thumbnail generation for STL, OBJ, and 3MF — browse your library visually instead of guessing from filenames.

🌀 Interactive 3D preview

Inspect any model in an orbiting 3D viewer, see real dimensions, and compare two models side by side.

🏷 Tags & collections

Tag files (in batches too) and group models into virtual collections — independent of where they live on disk.

📦 Archive browsing

Look inside .zip archives, preview the models in them, and act on them without extracting anything first.

🔒 Local by design

Your files never leave your machine. No uploads, no account, no subscription — just a desktop app and your folders.

Why local-first?

Cloud model managers want your STL collection on their servers — uploads, accounts, subscriptions, and your designs in someone else's hands.

Polytray indexes your existing folders in place. It works offline, it's as fast as your disk, and your library stays exactly where it is. Delete the app and nothing is lost or held hostage.
